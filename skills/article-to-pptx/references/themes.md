# Theme catalog

The builder includes the original `default` plus ten presets inspired by html-ppt. These are native PowerPoint compositions, with editable shapes and text. No CSS, animations or web fonts are required. All presets keep SF Pro for headings and body unless overridden.

Set `project.theme` to `{"preset":"midnight-lab"}`. Optional overrides are `primary`, `accent`, `background`, `foreground`, `surface`, `border`, and `fontFace`. Colors accept six hexadecimal digits with or without `#`. Omit old explicit colors when choosing a preset, or those overrides will take precedence.

## Selection and adaptation

During research, recommend two or three suitable presets and explain the audience/content fit. Include the selected preset and proposed layout treatments in the storyboard approval. The existing default remains available with red `#DC2626`; a theme selection changes the palette intentionally. Current brand instructions override saved preferences.

Presets change cover geometry, slide framing, and split-image placement. Nord field, terminal mint and cobalt atlas use vertical process timelines; the other themes use horizontal timelines. Content slide types remain compatible across presets. Use the theme composition as a starting point, then adapt native editable diagrams to the argument. Do not force a flow, timeline or decorative shape to imply facts the source does not support.

Check text/background and text/surface contrast after brand overrides, especially dark presets. Keep diagrams and screenshots contained; never crop evidence. Review all rendered slides for contrast, title wraps, visible image alignment and connector meaning. Font availability depends on the rendering machine.
